## Why AGPL-3.0 and not MIT or Apache-2.0

Those are permissive: a company can take the code, change it, and ship it
closed-source while only keeping a notice. AGPL-3.0 is the standard license that
keeps derived programs open, and it is recognized by GitHub, package managers,
and corporate license scanners — unlike a custom source-available text.
